### Groundwork

- Webpack does not buy you much here. An SDK is a single JS or TS file, and Webpack's strength is its loaders and plugins.
- Rollup is the first choice — it is built for bundling JavaScript. Also worth considering: esbuild (Go) and swc (Rust; a Babel replacement, with build speed in esbuild's league).
- The target is UMD, CMD, AMD, ES modules, and CommonJS2. Rollup is good at covering several module formats at once, so: microbundle.
