---
title: How a CDN Works
---

- A plain CDN does not touch a single byte of your files by default: it caches, distributes, and applies gzip / brotli. Automatic compression, format conversion and resizing are a separate **image CDN** capability (imgix, Cloudflare Images, Alibaba Cloud OSS image processing), usually billed separately — putting an image on a CDN does not mean it has been optimised; see [#69](https://github.com/Fzgt/Blog/issues/69).
- Even images that are not public front-page assets are worth putting on a CDN: a profile picture is private data, but other users still load it many times over, especially for popular accounts.
