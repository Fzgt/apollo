# Coreflow phone screen - self-introduction

Interview: 8/4, midday | Type: phone screen (getting to know each other)

---

## 1. Self-intro

### 1. Who I am

I'm a frontend/full-stack engineer with about 4 years of commercial experience, mostly working on high-traffic, large-scale consumer products — I've worked at Baidu, BioMap, and most recently an AI startup called BeyzAI.

---

### 2. Narrative thread — the range of scale

My experience spans different scales and stages of companies — at Baidu I worked on a product with over 100 million daily active users, at BioMap I led core architecture design at an early-stage startup, and at BeyzAI, an even earlier-stage AI startup, I independently owned a full feature end to end.

---

### 3. Proof point — the Baidu virtualized list

For example, at Baidu, I worked on performance optimization for the infinite-scroll feed on the app's homepage — a product with over 100 million daily active users. The original list implementation caused noticeable lag, so I designed and built a custom virtual list hook combined with IntersectionObserver for on-demand rendering. That reduced the number of DOM nodes on screen from over 500 to around 20, and improved the scroll frame rate from 30fps to a stable 60fps.

---

### 4. Tech stack

Overall, most of my experience is on the frontend — I'm strong in the React and Vue ecosystems, and I have a solid background in frontend performance optimization. I'm also interested in graphics/3D — I've worked on Web 3D projects. Beyond that, I'm comfortable working with Node.js-based frameworks and can independently handle backend development as well.

## 2. Why Coreflow

### Core answer (say this first — it maps to the product direction)

What really draws me to Coreflow is what you're actually building. Your work is closely tied to image and video content, which lines up directly with the performance work I did on Baidu's feed — I'd really like to keep solving that kind of problem at a large scale, because that complexity is a great growth opportunity for me.

---

### Follow-up answer (if a later question opens the door, or there is time left after the core answer)

I'm also drawn to the full-stack AI approach — training and deploying your own models across chat, image, and video, rather than just calling someone else's API. And I am excited about the stage you're at — a small team already serving millions of users daily. I'd love to be part of that journey from 0 to 1, and 1 to 100.

---

## How to use this

- The full self-intro (who I am + narrative thread + proof point) runs about 60-90 seconds
- If "Why Coreflow" is only asked once, run the core answer straight into the follow-up answer (about 40-50 seconds)
- If the interviewer splits it into two questions (say "why this role" / "why AI"), use the core answer for the first and the follow-up answer for the second, so nothing repeats
