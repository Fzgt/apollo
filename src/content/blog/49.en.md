## How to work through this

1. Do one thing first: cross off everything you could talk about for three minutes without preparation.
2. Split what is left into two piles — the ★ entries have to be filled in; the ○ entries only if there is time.
3. Among the ★ entries, pick out the **numbers** (500ms, 5M PV, 30→60 FPS, 2,000 QPS, 15%, 70%, 129→76, 2.6×, 1,020, 15 units, 406, 60 seconds, six events). You will always be asked how they were measured.
4. Only after that pass, move on to walking through the projects and mock follow-ups.
